How it works

You do not send money for something you have not already seen running. That is the whole shape of it.

  1. Write to me with your logo, your link, and your one line.
  2. I put it up. Usually the same day.
  3. You look at it on the live site. Open the studio and it is there in the corner.
  4. You have three days to decide. If you are happy, you pay, and it runs for a full month from the day it went up. The three days are not taken off your month.
  5. If you are not happy, say so or say nothing at all. It comes down on the fourth day and you owe me nothing.

What I promise

  • If your card does not run for the days you paid for, you get those days back or your money back. You choose which.

  • I will tell you how many times your card was pressed, whenever you ask.
  • Nothing about the people who see your card is collected, so nothing about them can be passed to you or to anyone else.

What I do not promise

This part is here because a page that only makes promises is not worth reading.

  • I will not quote you traffic numbers. This site is new and I cannot prove them, and a number I cannot prove is worth nothing to you.
  • There is no impression count. I count presses on your card and nothing else, on purpose, because counting views means writing a record on every page load.
  • No refund once a month has actually run, because it ran.

What to send me

  • A logo, as a PNG, a JPEG or a WebP, under 200 KB. Wide shapes work best. It is shown 32 pixels tall.
  • A link, starting with https://
  • One line of text, up to 60 characters. Optional.
  • The dates you want, or just say when you would like to start.

No animated files, and no SVG. An SVG can carry code, and a file that can carry code is not something to serve from a domain other people trust.

Your visitors

Your logo is stored on this site and served from this site. Nothing is loaded from your server, so no browser tells you who was here, and no ad network is involved at any point. That is deliberate and it is the reason this card can exist on a page that promises to collect nothing. The full note is at /privacy.
